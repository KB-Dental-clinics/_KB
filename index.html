About Me

Dentistry that explains itself.

I'm Dr. Kariem El Batouty, a general and cosmetic dentist practicing in Cairo. I founded KB Dental Clinics to give patients a calmer, more transparent kind of dental visit — one where every option is explained clearly before any treatment begins, and comfort is treated as seriously as clinical accuracy.

My work spans preventive care, restorative dentistry, cosmetic treatments, and smile design, and I keep both clinics equipped with modern sterilization and imaging standards. Whether it's a routine cleaning or a more involved procedure, my approach stays the same: listen first, plan carefully, and treat every patient the way I'd want my own family treated.

Outside the chair, I spend time keeping up with new techniques and materials in dentistry, so patients at Ard El Golf and Etrana Health Care City get care that reflects current best practice, not just habit.

Treatments

Care built around your bite.

A full range of dental care, from everyday maintenance to detailed restorative and cosmetic work — the same standard at both clinics.

General & Preventive Care

Check‑ups, professional cleaning, fillings, and early diagnosis to keep small issues from becoming big ones.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Teeth whitening, veneers, and smile design plans built around your natural bite and facial proportions.

Restorative Treatment

Crowns, bridges, and dental implants for patients rebuilding function and confidence in their bite.

Root Canal Therapy

Gentle, thorough endodontic treatment aimed at saving natural teeth wherever possible.

Orthodontic Guidance

Assessment and referral pathways for braces and clear aligners, coordinated with your treatment plan.

Pediatric Dentistry

Friendly, low‑pressure visits designed to help younger patients build good habits early.
